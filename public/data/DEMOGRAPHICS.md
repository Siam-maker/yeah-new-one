# Population and environmental snapshots

- **UN World Population Prospects 2024.** The single-year population-by-age-and-sex tables from the [wpp2024 distribution](https://github.com/PPgp/wpp2024/tree/main/data-raw) are based on the [UN release](https://population.un.org/wpp/). The build converts thousands of people to people, groups ages into five-year bands, and calculates median age by uniform interpolation inside one year of age. Annual growth compares consecutive midyear totals. The 1950–2023 series contains estimates, and 2024–2050 uses the UN medium variant. Small differences can result from rounding.
- **Gapminder Systema Globalis.** The [indicator metadata](https://github.com/open-numbers/ddf--gapminder--systema_globalis/blob/master/ddf--concepts.csv) attributes population density to UN World Population Prospects (release unspecified) and [urban share](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.URB.TOTL.IN.ZS) and [urban growth](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.URB.GROW) to World Bank World Development Indicators. Density divides population by land area. Urban definitions vary by reporting country. The snapshot preserves missing values and excludes these indicators for projection years. Population totals and urban percentages come from different statistical products, so the interface presents the urban/rural split as shares.
- **Natural Earth 1:110m country boundaries.** Generalized shapes were joined by ISO and UN M49 codes. The site includes 173 mapped areas. Small countries and territories can be absent. Country fills are national aggregates. The UN world total includes areas outside the map. Map boundaries do not constitute a statement about sovereignty.
- **Natural Earth 1:10m populated places.** POP_MAX is a mixed-date estimate based on the source’s settlement definitions. Selected places with reported estimates of at least 2 million appear in country profiles. Coverage and settlement definitions vary.
- **Historical sea level.** [EPA's Sea Level indicator](https://www.epa.gov/climate-indicators/climate-change-indicators-sea-level) attributes the reconstructed global record to CSIRO. The [archived CSV](https://github.com/datasets/sea-level-rise/blob/master/data/epa-sea-level.csv) provides the central estimate and lower/upper error bounds relative to 1880. The build converts inches to centimetres and retains the observed years 1880–2013. Local relative sea level, as recorded by a tide gauge, is a separate quantity.

The versioned JSON ships with the application. To regenerate it, run `python3 scripts/build-demographics.py INPUT_DIR ATLAS_INPUT_DIR` from the repository root. Place the UN mirror under `INPUT_DIR/wpp2024-main`, the Gapminder mirror under `INPUT_DIR/ddf--gapminder--systema_globalis-master`, the Natural Earth `ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson` at `INPUT_DIR/natural`, the sea-level archive at `INPUT_DIR/sea.csv`, and the optional Natural Earth populated-place GeoJSON at `ATLAS_INPUT_DIR/places.geojson`. Upstream releases may change and should be checked before refreshing the published snapshot.
